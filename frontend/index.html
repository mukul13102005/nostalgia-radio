ℹ️ About Nostalgia Radio

Nostalgia Radio ek multi-theme ambient radio website hai — har theme ka apna mood, apna playlist, aur apna live chat adda hota hai. Construction site ke Raju Mistri se lekar corporate cubicle tak, shaadi ki baraat se lekar highway ke bus driver tak — har vibe ka apna ghar hai yahan.

🎵 Gaane YouTube ke official embedded player ke through stream hote hain — is site par kuch bhi host nahi hota, saare rights unke asli owners (labels, composers, performers) ke paas rehte hain.

💬 Live chat mein jo bhi is waqt yahi theme sun raha hai, uske saath baat kar sakte ho.
